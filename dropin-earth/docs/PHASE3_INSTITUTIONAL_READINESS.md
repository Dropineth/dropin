Date: 2026-07-14

Audience: UN agencies, governments, NGOs, climate funds, research

## Current Position

CanopyProof now has a route-closed institutional trust foundation: durable
identity and organization authority, immutable evidence and verification,
append-only audit, governed methodology and proof records, a reviewed MRV
lineage graph, challenge authority, and a deterministic Environmental Proof
lifecycle projection. Disposable native PostgreSQL execution confirms those
core authorities can reject stale source roots, cross-tenant reads,
unauthorized mutation, and unresolved challenge reliance.

A governed ESG metric authority and canonical reporting authority now add
immutable metric-definition, metric-result, source-member, metric-member, and
report facts. Exact decimal values, uncertainty, independent human review,
current-source projection, and a fail-closed institutional consumer adapter
are bound into deterministic roots. The revised metric-bound path passes the
TypeScript, PGlite, and disposable native PostgreSQL gates. Both authorities
remain route-closed and preparation-only.
The mounted ESG routes still use a clearly labeled process-local compatibility
service and must not be used as institutional evidence.

This is engineering conformance evidence, not institutional accreditation.
No institution, satellite provider, regulator, standards body, or auditor has
approved the system for external reliance.

## Three-Layer Satellite Boundary

The baseline, observation, and audit records are deterministic protocol inputs:

1. Sentinel/Landsat fixture inputs can anchor a baseline root.
2. Planet/SAR fixture inputs can append observations and open a challenge when
   a bounded high-risk threshold is exceeded.
3. Maxar/Airbus/drone/field-validator fixture inputs can append an audit
   attestation.

Only an `AuditAttested` event can satisfy the protocol prerequisite for
`CertificateIssued`; no unresolved challenge may exist. This design does not
prove that any fixture came from the named provider. Live adapters, provider
signatures, licensing, calibration, cloud masking, temporal alignment, and
human audit operations remain future governed work.

The metadata-extraction adapter is a separate evidence-custody boundary, not a
satellite-provider authenticity claim. Its deterministic tests prove that one
allowlisted public key signed one minimized receipt and that E3c can preserve
that verification additively. They do not prove sensor truth, extractor
correctness, environmental outcomes, or live provider provenance. The adapter
and its HTTP route remain unmounted, and no modeled E3b row is mutable or
backfilled as verified.

## Reliance Rules

- An immutable issuance fact is not automatically current.
- `ACTIVE` means that the route-closed technical projection satisfied its
  current inputs at a stated time. It does not mean certified, regulated,
  tradable, tax eligible, or financially guaranteed.
- Challenges and revocations remain visible; they cannot be erased or hidden
  by reissuing a presentation object.
- AI, agents, and remote-sensing observations cannot approve themselves.
- Public and institutional consumers must receive minimized projections, not
  raw evidence, precise locations, private contact data, or credentials.

## Required Independent Reviews

Before institutional reliance, obtain documented approval from:

- security and privacy reviewers;
- scientific and remote-sensing methodology reviewers;
- community safeguarding and data-rights reviewers;
- jurisdiction-specific legal and claims counsel;
- independent verifier and audit operations owners; and
- production SRE, database, KMS/HSM, and incident-response owners.

## Activation Decision

Phase 3 remains blocked from production and institutional reliance. The next
review may change this decision only when each open gate has named evidence,
an accountable owner, an expiry date, an independently reproducible test, and
a rollback or governed supersession procedure. Deployment approval alone is
not institutional approval.
